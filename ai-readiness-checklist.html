The 6 Assessment Areas

What You'll Discover

The checklist evaluates your business across six areas where AI consistently delivers the biggest ROI. See where you stand — and where to act first.

Area 01

Customer Communication

Are you responding to leads fast enough? Businesses that respond within 5 minutes are 9x more likely to convert. Discover whether AI-powered chat and follow-up automation could plug your response gap — including after-hours coverage.

Area 02

Lead Generation & Conversion

Is your website turning visitors into customers — or quietly losing them? This section reveals whether AI-driven lead capture, qualification bots, and smart CTAs could dramatically increase the percentage of visitors who actually reach out.

Area 03

Content & Marketing

Are you consistently creating content that attracts customers? Most South Florida businesses publish inconsistently. AI can generate SEO blogs, social posts, and email campaigns on a schedule — without adding headcount.

Area 04

Operations & Workflow

How much manual work is slowing your team down? Scheduling, invoicing, reminders, data entry — these tasks consume hours every week. Find out exactly which workflows in your business are ripe for AI automation.

Area 05

Customer Experience

How personalized and responsive is your customer experience? AI lets even small businesses deliver enterprise-level personalization — from tailored recommendations to proactive follow-ups that feel human, not robotic.

Area 06

Data & Decision Making

Are you using data to drive business decisions — or going with gut instinct? AI tools can surface actionable insights from your existing data, helping you spot trends, predict demand, and make smarter moves faster.
