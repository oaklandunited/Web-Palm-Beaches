What makes Web Palm Beaches different from national AI firms?

We're local. We know Palm Beach County, we know the Jupiter market, and we're available for in-person meetings when needed. National agencies apply generic playbooks; we build solutions for your specific business, your specific industry, and your specific competitive environment.

How quickly will I see results?

Most clients see a working first milestone within 14 days. For focused projects like a booking automation or customer chatbot, you can be live within 3–4 weeks. ROI typically materializes within the first 60–90 days as time savings accumulate and the system starts generating leads or serving customers automatically.

Can you help with AI for a medical or healthcare practice?

Yes — and it's one of our specialties given the density of medical practices in northern Palm Beach County. We build HIPAA-compliant workflows, patient scheduling systems, and communication automations that respect the regulatory environment healthcare businesses operate in.

What's involved in a free strategy call?

It's a 30-minute conversation — no slides, no sales pitch. At the end, we tell you exactly what we'd recommend and what it would cost. You decide what to do from there.
