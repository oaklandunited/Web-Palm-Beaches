What's Actually Included in a Web Design Quote?

When you get a proposal from a web design agency, the price typically covers some combination of:

  • Custom design: Brand colors, typography, layouts — not a $49 template
  • Development: Clean code, mobile responsiveness, and page speed optimization
  • Content: Copywriting for service pages, homepage, and about page
  • On-page AEO/GEO: Meta tags, heading structure, image alt text, and schema markup
  • Integrations: Contact forms, booking systems, CRM connections
  • Post-launch support: Bug fixes and training within the first 30–60 days

Budget quotes often exclude content creation, ongoing AEO/GEO, and hosting. Always ask what's included before signing anything.

Palm Beach County reality check: A $1,200 website from a marketplace freelancer might look fine at first glance but often has poor page speed, zero local AEO/GEO optimization, and no schema markup. The Hidden Costs Nobody Mentions

The upfront build price is just the beginning. Budget for:

  • Hosting: $20–$150/month depending on speed and reliability needs
  • Domain: $15–$50/year
  • Maintenance: $150–$500/month for updates, security, and backups
  • Ongoing AEO/GEO: $500–$2,000/month if you want to consistently rank
  • Content updates: Blog posts, landing pages, seasonal promotions

DIY vs. Agency: The Real Math

Squarespace costs $276/year. A professional agency site costs $5,000+. The difference seems obvious — until you factor in your time (20–60 hours to build a DIY site competently), conversion rate losses from generic templates, and the ongoing frustration of managing a platform not built for your business.

Most business owners who've tried DIY and then hired professionals describe it as their "I wish I'd done this sooner" moment. The conversion rate improvement alone typically justifies the investment within 6–12 months.

5 Questions to Ask Before Signing a Web Design Contract

  1. What Core Web Vitals scores do you target at launch?
  2. Is on-page AEO/GEO included — not just meta tags, but schema markup and keyword structure?
  3. Who owns the design files, code, and domain after the project?
  4. What does ongoing support cost and exactly what does it include?
  5. Can I see three recent client sites built in the past 12 months?

If they can't answer those confidently, keep looking.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a basic website cost in Palm Beach County?
A basic 5–8 page informational website from a professional agency in Palm Beach County typically costs $3,000–$6,000. This includes custom design, mobile responsiveness, on-page AEO/GEO, and contact forms. DIY website builders are cheaper but require significant time and often underperform on search engines.
What affects the cost of a website?
The main factors are: number of pages, level of custom design, required functionality (e-commerce, booking systems, AI chatbots), whether copy and content creation is included, and ongoing maintenance scope. AI integrations, custom databases, and third-party API connections add significantly to the cost.
Is it cheaper to use a website builder or hire a web design agency?
Website builders cost $16–$50/month but require 20–60 hours of your time and produce generic results with limited AEO/GEO performance. A professional agency costs more upfront but delivers a faster, better-converting site. Most businesses find the investment pays back within 6–12 months through better lead generation and conversions.
How much does website maintenance cost per month?
Website maintenance in South Florida typically runs $150–$500/month depending on scope. This covers security updates, software patches, backups, uptime monitoring, minor content changes, and technical support. Neglecting maintenance leads to security vulnerabilities, broken functionality, and ranking drops.
What should I expect to pay for an ecommerce website in Florida?
An ecommerce site with a product catalog, shopping cart, and payment processing typically costs $6,000–$20,000 depending on product count, inventory complexity, and required integrations like shipping, accounting, and CRM. Custom configurators or subscription models add to the scope.
