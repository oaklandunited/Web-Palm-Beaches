FDUTPA & Fair Housing

Compliant & Legally Protected AI

Florida real estate marketing is strictly regulated by the Florida Deceptive and Unfair Trade Practices Act (FDUTPA). AI-generated listing copy and virtually staged images must follow explicit disclosure guidelines to avoid misrepresentation fines.

Additionally, we verify that your automated lead scoring and routing algorithms comply fully with federal and state Fair Housing rules, preventing algorithmic discrimination.

The Real Estate Shield

We deploy standard guardrails for local brokerages:

  • FDUTPA Disclosure Checks: Automatically flag and add text watermarks to virtually staged listing images.
  • Fair Housing Audits: Filter copy options to ensure zero discriminatory language in AI-generated copy.
  • Private LLM Tunnels: Ensure listing data, purchase agreements, and client details are never shared with public models.
FAQ

Real Estate AI Frequently Asked Questions

How do your systems ensure compliance with Florida's virtual staging rules?
Florida MLS and FDUTPA require explicit disclosure if photos are virtually staged. Our system automatically appends compliant watermarks and text disclosures (e.g., "This image is virtually staged") to all AI-altered listings before publication.
Can the AI integrate with MLS and dotloop/DocuSign?
Yes. We build secure API integrations that connect our AI agents with local MLS databases (Matrix/Stellar MLS) and transaction management platforms like dotloop, DocuSign, or SkySlope, syncing contract documents and listing data seamlessly.
How does speed-to-lead automation work for inbound real estate leads?
When a lead registers on Zillow, Realtor.com, or your website, our AI agent texts and emails them within 60 seconds to qualify their timeline, budget, and pre-approval status, booking a showing directly into your agent's calendar.
Is client contact information kept private?
Yes. All client communication and lead details are processed through secure, encrypted connections. We sign data processing agreements and ensure no client contact data is shared or used to train public LLM models.
