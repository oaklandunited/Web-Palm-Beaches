Our Story

20 Years of Web.
Now AI-First.

Web Palm Beaches grew out of two decades of hands-on work building websites, running digital marketing campaigns, and solving real technology problems for businesses across Palm Beach County. We watched the digital landscape shift from static HTML pages to complex CMS platforms — and now to a world where artificial intelligence can fundamentally change how a business operates.

In 2022, something changed. The AI tools we had been quietly experimenting with started producing results that were impossible to ignore — chatbots that handled genuine customer conversations, automation pipelines that eliminated dozens of hours of manual work per week, analytics that revealed patterns no human analyst had caught. We had a decision to make: offer AI as one more line item on a proposal, or rebuild the practice around it entirely.

We chose the latter. Today, every engagement we take on is designed from the ground up around AI-powered workflows, intelligent agents, and measurable automation — layered on top of the web and SEO expertise we've spent 20 years refining. That combination is what our competitors can't replicate.

2022

FAQ

Common Questions About Working With Us

Answers to what most clients want to know before they book their first call.

Where is Web Palm Beaches based?

We're based in Palm Beach County, Florida, and serve businesses throughout South Florida — West Palm Beach, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Jupiter, and the surrounding communities. We also work with clients remotely across Florida and nationally for the right projects.

How long has the company been in business?

We have over 20 years of combined experience in web development and digital marketing. Web Palm Beaches in its current AI-focused form has been operating in Palm Beach County for several years, and we have been building AI and automation systems for local businesses since the technology became mature enough to deliver real ROI.

What industries do you specialize in?

We work across many industries but have deep specialization in law firms, medical and dental practices, real estate, restaurants, and home service contractors in South Florida. Each of these industries has specific compliance considerations, workflow patterns, and customer communication needs that we've built dedicated experience and templates around.

Do you work with startups or only established businesses?

Both. For startups, we help build the digital infrastructure from the ground up — website, AI chatbot, lead capture, and CRM — so you launch with a competitive presence. For established businesses, we layer AI and automation on top of what's already working to eliminate bottlenecks and accelerate growth. The approach differs, but the commitment to ROI is the same.

What does a typical engagement look like?

We start with a free 30-minute strategy call to understand your business, goals, and challenges. From there, we conduct an operational audit (typically 1 week), deliver a prioritized roadmap, and begin building once you approve the scope. Most projects launch a working first milestone within 2–4 weeks of kickoff. We stay involved after launch for support, optimization, and growth.

Can I see examples of your previous work?

Yes — our case studies page has detailed breakdowns of client projects including the challenge, our approach, the technology we used, and the measurable results. We're proud of the outcomes our clients achieve and happy to share specific examples relevant to your industry on a strategy call.
