FAQ

Questions from Delray Beach business owners

I'm a small business — is AI really relevant for me?

Absolutely. In fact, small businesses often see the biggest impact from AI because the time savings are so significant relative to team size. Automating your booking system, customer follow-up, or review management can free up 10–20 hours a week for a solo operator or small team — time you can put into growth.

How do you handle seasonal fluctuations common in Delray Beach?

We design AI systems that scale with your business. During peak season, your chatbot handles the surge in inquiries without hiring extra staff. During slower periods, automated campaigns keep your pipeline warm. The system works year-round without you having to manage it manually.

What if I don't have a big budget?

We work with budgets starting from $2,500 for focused, high-impact automation. We always start by identifying which single automation will deliver the fastest payback — so your first project pays for itself before we move to the next. You don't have to commit to a massive engagement upfront.

Will I need to learn complicated software?

No. We build systems that run in the background and require minimal management. What we do build you to use is simple — dashboards, brief daily check-ins, or just reading a summary email. We also provide full training for your team and ongoing support so you're never on your own.

Do you work with businesses in surrounding areas like Boynton Beach or Deerfield Beach?

Yes — we serve all of Palm Beach County and South Florida broadly. We have pages for Boca Raton, West Palm Beach, and Jupiter, and we welcome businesses from throughout the region.
