The Case For Your Own Website

Why Every Contractor Needs a Lead-Generating Website

01

Lead Aggregators Kill Your Margins

Angi, HomeAdvisor, and Thumbtack charge per lead — whether you close the job or not. And you're competing against 3–4 other contractors on every single lead. Your own website generates exclusive leads at a fraction of the cost.

02

"Near Me" Searches Are Exploding

"Roofer near me," "electrician West Palm Beach," "kitchen remodel Fort Lauderdale" — these searches happen thousands of times per month in South Florida. Without a website optimized for them, those leads go to your competitors who do.

03

Your Work Speaks for Itself

You do great work — but if it's only visible on your truck and your business card, most potential customers will never know. A website with a professional project gallery is the single best sales tool you can have.

What We Deliver

Everything Your Contractor Website Needs to Generate Leads

01

Service Area Pages

Dedicated pages targeting every city and neighborhood you serve. "Roof repair Jupiter FL." "Kitchen remodeling Boca Raton." "AC repair Palm Beach Gardens." Each page targets a specific local keyword and drives qualified traffic — this is the playbook that consistently gets contractors into the Google Map Pack.

02

Before/After Project Gallery

Nothing sells contractor services like visual proof. We build filterable galleries showcasing your best work — organized by service type, with before/after sliders that make the transformation undeniable. Customers who see your work before calling are already sold.

03

AI-Powered Lead Capture

An intelligent chatbot that engages visitors immediately — asking what type of project, their timeline, property address, and whether they'd like a free estimate. Leads are captured and sent to your phone via text and email instantly. No more missed opportunities while you're on a job site.

04

Google Maps & AI-powered Local Search (SEO/GEO)

Full Google Business Profile optimization, local citation building across 50+ directories, review generation automation, HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema markup, and NAP consistency across the web. The local map pack is where contractor leads come from — we get you there.

05

Automated Review Collection

After every completed job, your system automatically sends a text asking for a Google review. More reviews = higher Maps ranking = more leads. We've seen contractors go from 15 reviews to 100+ in six months with this system — and their phone starts ringing on its own.

06

Estimate Request System

Structured quote request forms that capture service needed, property type and location, project description, photos (yes — customers can upload photos of their problem), and preferred contact method. Delivered to you in a clean format by email, text, or into your CRM.

07

Mobile-Optimized Design

Your potential customers are on their phone — standing in their kitchen looking at a broken faucet, or outside after a storm. Your site needs to load fast and make it dead simple to call or request a quote from any device.
