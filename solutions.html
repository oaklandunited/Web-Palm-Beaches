FAQ

Common Questions About AI Solutions

Straight answers to the questions we hear most often from South Florida business owners exploring AI for the first time.

What kinds of businesses benefit most from AI solutions?

Any business that handles repetitive tasks, manages leads, books appointments, or communicates with customers regularly. We've seen the strongest ROI in service businesses — law firms, medical practices, real estate, contractors, and restaurants — where time savings and lead response speed directly translate to revenue. If you're spending hours on tasks that follow the same pattern, AI can likely handle them.

Do I need existing technology infrastructure to get started?

No. We work with businesses at every stage — from companies with no digital tools to those with complex tech stacks. We start with an operational audit to understand exactly what you have, identify gaps, and recommend the right tools. Many clients start with zero AI infrastructure and are fully operational within 4–8 weeks.

How is Web Palm Beaches different from other AI consultants?

We build and implement — not just advise. Many consultants deliver a strategy deck and walk away. We stay involved through every phase: discovery, build, testing, launch, and ongoing optimization. We're also South Florida locals who understand the unique dynamics of Palm Beach County businesses, not a national firm that treats you as a ticket number.

How quickly will I see results from an AI implementation?

Faster than most people expect. Simple automations like AI review requests or lead follow-up sequences often show measurable results within the first 30 days. More complex systems — full AI agents or multi-step automation workflows — typically reach full performance within 60–90 days as they accumulate data and we tune their behavior based on real usage.

What's the minimum budget to get started with AI?

Focused projects — a single AI chatbot, an automated follow-up sequence, or a review request workflow — start at around $2,500. Comprehensive AI strategy and full implementation typically ranges from $10,000–$50,000 depending on scope. We always start with a free strategy call to scope your project honestly and prioritize the changes with the highest ROI first.

Will AI solutions require ongoing management from my team?

Minimal. We build systems designed to run independently, and we provide training so your team knows how to monitor and update them without relying on us for every change. We also offer optional monthly support retainers for businesses that want us to handle ongoing optimization, updates, and performance reporting.
