Accessibility Statement

Web Palm Beaches is dedicated to ensuring that our digital resources are accessible to individuals with disabilities. We strive to provide an inclusive digital environment that allows all visitors to gather information, explore AI solutions, and coordinate services regardless of physical or cognitive capability.

We are actively working to align our web presence with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA standards, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

1. Core Accessibility Actions

We implement accessibility-first practices throughout our web development lifecycle, including:

  • Semantic HTML Markup: Constructing logical document outlines (appropriate headings, sections, and tags) to ensure clean screen-reader translation.
  • Form Labeling: Ensuring all intake elements, checkboxes, and buttons have explicit text labels or ARIA descriptors.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Maintaining visible focus rings and logical tab orders so that users can navigate all pages without a pointer device.
  • Color Contrast: Verifying that all visual content (text, icons, forms) maintains high contrast levels against background canvases, meeting WCAG requirements.
  • Alt Text: Providing text-alternatives for all high-value contextual images and media assets.

2. Ongoing Assessments and Auditing

Digital standards shift. To ensure persistent WCAG compliance, we perform regular automated accessibility sweeps, evaluate color contrast levels, and conduct manual keyboard focus reviews across our static and dynamic page elements.

3. Reporting Barriers & Contacting Us

If you encounter a barrier, find a page layout difficult to navigate, or experience screen-reader translation issues, please contact us immediately. We will work to resolve the issue or provide the information through an alternative format:
