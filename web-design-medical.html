FAQ

Common Questions from Medical Practices

Is the AI chatbot HIPAA compliant?

Our chatbot is designed to not collect protected health information (PHI). It answers general questions and captures basic contact details for scheduling. For PHI-sensitive communications, we recommend using your existing patient portal. We're transparent about this distinction from day one.

Can you integrate with our practice management system?

Yes. We work with most major systems including Athenahealth, DrChrono, eClinicalWorks, Kareo, and Dentrix. We also integrate with scheduling tools like Zocdoc and Calendly Health. If you use a different system, let us know — we'll confirm compatibility before the project starts.

Do you write the medical content?

We create AEO/GEO-optimized content drafts for your services and conditions, subject to your clinical review and approval. All medical content must be reviewed and approved by a licensed provider before publishing. We provide the drafts and structure — your clinical team ensures accuracy.

How do you handle patient reviews on the website?

We integrate your Google reviews directly into the site and can set up a review widget showing your rating and recent feedback. We also build automated review request workflows that send a text after appointments — making it effortless for satisfied patients to share their experience.

Will the website work for multiple office locations?

Absolutely. We build multi-location architectures with location-specific pages, individual Google Business Profile optimization, and centralized content management so your team can update the site without touching code. Each location gets its own AEO & GEO presence while sharing your brand identity.
