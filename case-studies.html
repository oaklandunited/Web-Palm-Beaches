The med spa's client satisfaction scores were excellent — NPS of 72 — but retention was a recurring problem. Over 60% of first-time clients didn't rebook within 60 days. The team knew the issue wasn't dissatisfaction; clients were simply busy and forgot. There was no systematic way to stay in touch post-visit. Staff were too occupied during peak hours to manually follow up, and a 90-day lapse typically meant a client was lost entirely.

What We Built

  • Post-visit thank-you sequence — personalized SMS 3 hours after appointment, includes a direct rebooking link with suggested next visit date based on treatment type
  • 30-day check-in flow — AI-generated message with a personalized treatment recommendation based on service history
  • Birthday reward automation — triggered 30 days before a client's birthday with a special offer, driving visits during an otherwise slow period
  • 90-day reactivation campaign — three-touch sequence for clients who haven't visited in 3+ months, with escalating incentives
  • Service-specific rebooking cadences — different follow-up timing for Botox (12 weeks), facials (4 weeks), laser treatments (6 weeks)

The Results

Within the first 90 days, rebooking rates improved from 38% to 51% — a 35% lift. The 90-day reactivation campaign brought back 28% of dormant clients in its first run, generating over $3,200 in a single campaign. Monthly revenue increased by $6,500 on average — entirely from clients who already knew and trusted the business. The system reached full ROI in under three weeks.

"We were sitting on a goldmine of happy clients who just needed a reason to come back. The AI sequences do that for us automatically. We added over $6,000 a month without spending a dollar on new client acquisition."
Owner — Medical Spa, Boca Raton, FL

Technologies Used

  • Klaviyo — email automation flows and segmentation
  • Attentive — SMS rebooking and reactivation sequences
  • AI personalization layer — service-type-specific message generation
  • Make (Integromat) — workflow orchestration and trigger management
