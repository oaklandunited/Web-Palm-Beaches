With Web Palm Beaches, you get a full team of AI strategists, developers, and specialists without the overhead of salaries, benefits, and management. We deploy faster, cost less than an in-house hire, and bring cross-industry experience that a single hire can't match.

Can you integrate AI with the software we already use?

Yes — this is one of our core specialties. We integrate with Salesforce, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Clio, practice management software, and hundreds of other platforms. We start by auditing your current tech stack, then build AI on top of it rather than replacing what works.

Is AI appropriate for regulated industries like finance and healthcare?

Absolutely. We have deep experience building compliant AI workflows for financial services and healthcare. We design systems that respect HIPAA requirements, data privacy obligations, and industry-specific compliance needs. We never compromise on security or compliance.
