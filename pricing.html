01

We scope every project in detail before quoting. What we quote is what you pay — the only way the price changes is if you change the scope, and we'll tell you upfront if you do.

Our Method

How We Price Projects

Every quote we send is the result of a real scoping conversation — not a price generator or a guessed estimate. Here's exactly how we arrive at a number.

  • 1

    Discovery Call First

    We don't quote blind. Every project starts with a 30-minute call where we understand your goals, existing tech, timeline, and what success actually looks like for you.

  • 2

    Scope Document

    Before a dollar amount, you receive a clear written scope: pages, features, integrations, deliverables, and milestones. No surprises, no "that wasn't included."

  • 3

    Fixed Price Quote

    Based on scope, complexity, and timeline, we send a fixed-price proposal. That number doesn't change unless the scope changes — and we'll tell you before we touch anything new.

  • 4

    Milestone-Based Payments

    We split payments into milestones tied to real deliverables. Most projects are 50% to kickoff, 50% at launch. You pay for work done — never in advance of results.

  • 5

    No Retainer Lock-In

    Ongoing plans are month-to-month. If we're not delivering value you can measure, you shouldn't be paying us. We earn the relationship every month.

Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

Questions we hear on almost every strategy call — answered plainly.

Can I start with a smaller package and upgrade later?

Yes. Many clients start with a Starter site to get to market quickly, then reinvest in a Professional redesign once they've validated their messaging. We build with clean, documented code that makes future upgrades efficient — not a rebuild from scratch. We'll also be upfront if your initial requirements suggest you'll need the higher tier to avoid a costly migration later.

Do you work with clients outside of South Florida?

We do. While our roots and specialization are in Palm Beach County and the greater South Florida market, we work with clients across the U.S. and internationally for web design and AI projects. Strategy calls, design reviews, and project management are fully remote-capable. That said, if you're local to the area, we're happy to meet in person.

What if my project scope changes mid-build?

Scope changes happen — that's normal. When they do, we pause, discuss the change, and issue a formal change order with a revised price and timeline before doing any additional work. You'll never get a surprise invoice. Minor changes within the spirit of the original scope are often absorbed without additional charge; substantive additions are quoted transparently.

Are your AI integrations compatible with my existing tools?

Almost certainly. Our AI and automation work is built on flexible, API-first tools that connect with virtually any modern CRM, scheduling platform, EHR, e-commerce system, or communication tool. During discovery we audit your existing stack and design integrations that work with what you already have — we don't force migrations or new software purchases unless there's a compelling reason.

What's included in the post-launch support period?

Post-launch support covers bug fixes, minor content adjustments, browser and device compatibility issues, and any functionality that isn't performing as scoped. It does not include new features, redesigns, or content production — those are handled through a maintenance plan or a new project scope. Most clients transition to our Website Maintenance retainer after the included support period to stay covered long-term.
