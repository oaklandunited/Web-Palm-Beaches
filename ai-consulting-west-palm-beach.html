FAQ

Common questions from West Palm Beach businesses

Do I need to be a tech company to benefit from AI?

Not at all. Our most impactful work is with traditional businesses — law firms, real estate agencies, medical practices, and restaurants — that have no technical staff. We handle everything and train your team on using the tools we build.

How long does a typical AI project take in West Palm Beach?

Most projects reach a working first milestone within 14 days of kickoff. A full automation system or AI agent typically launches within 4–8 weeks. We can also start with a single quick-win automation to show value fast before expanding.

What does AI consulting cost for a small business?

Projects start from $2,500 for focused automation or chatbot work. Comprehensive AI strategy and implementation typically ranges from $10,000–$50,000. We'll scope a solution that matches your budget and prioritizes the highest-ROI improvements first.

Do you work with businesses outside of West Palm Beach?

Yes — while we're based in Palm Beach County, we serve clients throughout South Florida and nationally. All of our work can be delivered remotely. We also serve Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Jupiter, and surrounding communities.

Will you handle the ongoing maintenance after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing support, monitoring, and optimization as part of our post-launch service. AI systems improve over time when properly maintained, and we stay involved to make sure your investment keeps growing in value.
