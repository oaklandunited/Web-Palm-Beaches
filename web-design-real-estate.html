FAQ

Common Questions from Real Estate Professionals

Which MLS systems do you integrate with?

We work with all major Florida MLS systems including BeachesMLS, Miami MLS (MIAMI Association), and Stellar MLS. IDX integration is included in every real estate package — there's no additional setup cost or separate license fee on our end.

Can I customize how listings appear on my site?

Yes. We design custom listing templates that match your brand — not the generic IDX look. Featured listings, new listings, and price-reduced properties can be highlighted automatically. You get a unique visual experience that reinforces your brand at every touch point.

How do neighborhood guides help me get more leads?

Buyers research neighborhoods before contacting agents. A guide ranking for "best neighborhoods in Boca Raton" or "living in Jupiter FL" brings hundreds of in-market buyers to your site each month — where they find your listings, your profile, and your contact form. It's the highest-ROI content investment most agents can make.

Can the AI chatbot connect to my CRM?

Yes. We integrate with Follow Up Boss, KvCORE, Lofty, BoomTown, and most real estate CRMs. Leads flow directly into your pipeline with qualification notes — timeline, budget, buyer vs. seller, neighborhoods of interest — so you know exactly who to call first.

Do you help with Google Ads for real estate?

Our focus is organic marketing — AEO/GEO, content, and AI-powered lead capture — which delivers the best cost-per-lead over time. That said, we can build landing pages optimized for Google Ads campaigns and connect you with trusted PPC partners in the South Florida market.
