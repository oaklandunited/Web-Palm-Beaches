The Problem

The Problem With Most Law Firm Websites

Most legal sites share the same four fatal flaws. If your firm's website has any of these, you're losing clients every day to competitors who don't.

01

They Look the Same

Dark blue, stock photos of gavels and handshakes, generic "About Our Firm" copy. Your website should reflect what makes your practice different — not blend into a sea of identical legal sites.

02

They Don't Capture Leads

A contact form buried on a separate page isn't a lead capture strategy. The average website visitor spends 54 seconds on a page. If you don't engage them fast, they're gone — and calling your competitor.

03

They're Invisible on Google

Having a website isn't enough. If you don't rank for terms like "personal injury lawyer West Palm Beach" or "family law attorney Boca Raton," your site might as well not exist.

04

They Can't Answer After Hours

A potential client searching for a DUI attorney at 11 PM on a Saturday isn't going to wait until Monday morning. If your site can't help them right now, someone else's will.

FAQ

Questions From Law Firms We've Worked With

How long does it take to build a law firm website?
Most law firm sites launch in 5–8 weeks from kickoff. Complex sites with multiple practice areas, custom AI features, and multi-attorney profiles may take 8–12 weeks. We'll give you a firm timeline during your strategy call before any work begins.
Can you help us rank for "[practice area] lawyer [city]"?
Yes — that's the central goal of every practice area page we build. Every site includes foundational AEO & GEO, and we offer ongoing monthly AEO & GEO retainer packages specifically for law firms targeting high-intent local legal keywords and AI queries. We focus on the searches and citations that bring in actual consultation requests, not just traffic.
Is the AI chatbot compliant with attorney advertising rules?
Yes. We configure the chatbot specifically to follow Florida Bar advertising guidelines. It doesn't provide legal advice — it qualifies inquiries, captures contact information, and connects potential clients with your intake team. We've built compliant intake systems for multiple South Florida firms.
Do you handle content writing for practice area pages?
We can. Content creation is available as an add-on service, or we can work efficiently with copy you provide. For legal content specifically, we recommend attorney review of all published material before launch — we build that review step into our process at no extra charge.
Can the chatbot integrate with our case management software?
In most cases, yes. We integrate directly with Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther, Lawmatics, and other major legal CRMs. The chatbot captures intake information and routes it — with lead scoring — into your existing workflows. Custom integrations are available for less common platforms.
