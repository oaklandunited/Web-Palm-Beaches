FAQ

Common Questions

Do participants need any coding experience?
None at all. Our training is built for non-technical business owners and staff. We use no-code tools like Zapier, Make, and ChatGPT that are designed to be used without programming knowledge. The focus is on strategic thinking and workflow design, not writing code.
How many people can attend a workshop?
The AI Quick Start fits up to 5 participants. The AI Team Immersion fits up to 10. The System Architect Program fits up to 5 for the intensive format. For larger teams, we offer custom enterprise workshops — contact us to discuss your needs and we'll build a custom curriculum.
Are the workshops in-person or virtual?
Both options are available. In-person workshops are held at your location anywhere in Palm Beach County, or at our preferred venue in West Palm Beach. Virtual workshops are conducted via Zoom with screen sharing and live building sessions. Most clients prefer in-person for the collaborative energy, but virtual works just as well for distributed teams.
What tools will my team actually use after training?
The core stack we teach is: ChatGPT and Claude for writing, research, and analysis; Zapier or Make for workflow automation; Notion AI for documentation and knowledge management; and Google Workspace AI for daily productivity. We customize the tool selection based on your industry and existing software — no team gets a generic curriculum.
What's the difference between training and Done-For-You implementation?
Done-For-You means we build everything and you use the outputs. Training means your team learns to build and manage AI workflows themselves. Many clients do both — they start with a Done-For-You implementation to get immediate results, then add training so their team can extend and maintain the systems going forward. The combination is the most powerful approach.
